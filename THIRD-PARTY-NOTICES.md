Package license files are retained with the published app in `LICENSES/`.

Official projects: https://github.com/dotnet/wpf,
https://github.com/dotnet/efcore, https://github.com/ericsink/SQLitePCL.raw,

no real Goodreads data or third-party cover art is bundled.
